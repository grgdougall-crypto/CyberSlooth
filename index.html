                03 Why This Exists

                The Larger Question

                The interesting challenge is not simply asking AI questions.

                CyberSlooth explores whether a constrained research system can:

                • 01 Discover something without being directly told what to find
                • 02 Follow promising leads
                • 03 Preserve provenance
                • 04 Distinguish observation from uncertainty
                • 05 Build useful memory over time

                These capabilities are the subject of the experiment—not claims about this prototype.

                04 Guardrails / Future Model

                Future guardrails

                CyberSlooth may eventually

                • Discover public pages
                • Follow links
                • Summarize findings
                • Rank interesting leads
                • Preserve research history

                CyberSlooth should not

                • Publish unsupported claims
                • Hide provenance
                • Treat speculation as fact
                • Crawl indefinitely
                • Silently modify archived findings
